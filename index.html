Synopsis

Manaswini grew up in a stone-and-slate house in the hills of Kumaon, and then went to Delhi University and learned a new vocabulary: independence, choice, feminism. She holds on to it hard. When her parents fix her marriage, she runs away in the middle of the night.

In Delhi, an NGO called Aanchal gives her a job and a purpose. A pub gives her Satya, a software engineer with a level, unreadable gaze. She refuses marriage on principle. She agrees to live with him instead, and for a while that feels like freedom.

Then a car explodes in a crowded market, and the people around her begin to vanish. The man she chose is not answering the questions she is starting to ask. Whether she stays, and why she chose him at all, is what this story slowly, and dangerously, works out.

Main Characters

Manaswini

A young woman from a village near Berinag, in Pithoragarh. She mistakes everything her parents stand for as a cage, and has not yet learned to tell tradition from wrong.

Satya

A software engineer in Delhi, level-headed and hard to read. He asks very little of her, and tells her even less.

Vishesh

Satya's friend, and a Delhi Police officer. He appears to be on the sidelines of Manaswini's story, and that is a mistake.

Genres & Themes

Genres: Thriller, Crime, Romance, Social Drama

Themes: Freedom versus Belonging, Trust and Suspicion, Family and Forgiveness, Ideals versus Reality, Love and Danger
